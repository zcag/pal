## spanish-words.json: CC BY-SA 4.0

Converted (`tools/spanish.ts`) from **6001 Spanish** by Jeff Doozan
(https://github.com/doozan/6001_Spanish, the build's notes.csv at commit
3ab6b9e), which is made of:

- **The word list**: hermitdave/FrequencyWords
  (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, OpenSubtitles 2018), CC
  BY-SA 4.0, lemmatised in doozan/spanish_data
  (https://github.com/doozan/spanish_data), whose counts also give each
  card its `weight`.
- **Meanings, genders and parts of speech**: English Wiktionary
  (https://en.wiktionary.org), by its contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Example sentences**: Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR,
  from the lists reviewed by CK and arh; every contributor is credited in
  6001 Spanish's CREDITS
  (https://github.com/doozan/6001_Spanish/blob/master/CREDITS).

The file as a whole is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): you may share and adapt
it, with credit, under the same license.
